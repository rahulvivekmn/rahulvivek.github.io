How My Photography Journey Began

Every photograph has a story. This is the story behind mine.

I never really thought that photography would become such an important part of my life. Like many people, I started by simply enjoying the moments around me — the places I visited, the people I met, and the little details that often go unnoticed in our busy lives.

But somewhere along the way, I began to see the world a little differently.

The way sunlight falls through a window. The reflection of city lights on a rainy street. The quiet beauty of a sunset by the beach. The genuine smile of someone lost in a moment.

I started noticing these little things, and I wanted to capture them.

That was the beginning of my photography journey.

A Different Perspective on Life

My professional life has always been in the world of software engineering. I spend much of my time solving problems, building applications, and thinking through lines of code.

Photography introduced me to a different way of thinking.

Behind a camera, there is no single right answer. Sometimes, the most beautiful photograph comes from an unexpected moment, an imperfect frame, or a little bit of patience.

I began to appreciate the process of slowing down, observing my surroundings, and waiting for the right moment.

For me, photography became a creative space — a place where I could step away from the structured world of technology and express myself in a completely different way.

And in an interesting way, the two worlds have more in common than I first imagined. Both require curiosity, attention to detail, experimentation, and the willingness to keep learning.

Discovering the Beauty of Kochi

Living in Kochi has given me endless inspiration.

There is something special about this city. Its streets, its coastline, its changing light, and the everyday lives of the people who call it home all have stories worth telling.

I find myself drawn to the contrast between the old and the new — quiet streets beside busy roads, traditional architecture surrounded by modern buildings, and the calm of the sea against the energy of the city.

Sometimes, inspiration comes from a planned photography outing. Other times, it appears unexpectedly while I'm simply walking around with my camera.

A beautiful patch of light. A fleeting expression. A reflection after the rain.

These are the moments that remind me why I love photography.

Kochi isn't just where I live and take photographs. It is a place that continues to shape the way I see the world.

Learning to See, Not Just to Shoot

When I first started taking photography seriously, I realised that owning a camera was only the beginning.

There was so much to learn — understanding light, composition, exposure, focal lengths, and the relationship between a subject and its surroundings.

I experimented, made mistakes, tried different settings, and slowly began to understand what worked for me.

But one of the biggest lessons was that a great photograph doesn't always depend on having the perfect equipment.

It begins with the way you see.

I started paying more attention to light and shadows, to the emotions in a person's expression, and to the atmosphere of a place. I learned that sometimes, waiting a little longer can make all the difference.

Every photograph became an opportunity to learn something new.

And that learning never really ends.

Finding My Own Style

Over time, I discovered the kinds of photographs that speak to me the most.

I'm drawn to portraits that feel natural and honest, candid moments that happen without direction, and cityscapes that carry a certain mood.

I love the atmosphere of a city at night — the warm glow of streetlights, reflections on wet roads, and the little pockets of life that appear after the sun goes down.

I also love the simplicity of natural light, especially during golden hour, when even an ordinary place can feel extraordinary.

Rather than chasing perfection, I want my photographs to feel real. I want them to hold on to the emotion of a moment and bring it back whenever someone looks at them.

I'm still discovering my style, and I think that's part of the beauty of this journey. There is always something new to explore, a different perspective to try, or a story waiting to be captured.

More Than Just a Hobby

As I spent more time with my camera, photography gradually became more than something I did in my free time.

It became a way to express myself.

A way to notice things I might otherwise have missed.

A way to preserve memories that would have disappeared with time.

And a way to connect with people, places, and stories beyond my everyday routine.

There is a unique feeling in looking at a photograph and remembering exactly how a moment felt — the light, the atmosphere, the emotion, and everything that made it special.

That feeling is what keeps me coming back to photography.

The Journey Is Still Unfolding

I don't consider myself someone who has figured everything out. I'm still learning, experimenting, and finding new ways to tell stories through my photographs.

There are places I want to explore, people I want to photograph, and countless moments I hope to capture.

Through this website, I want to share more than just my favourite images. I want to share the stories behind them, the experiences that shaped them, and the lessons I discover along the way.

If you're here because you love photography, appreciate visual stories, or simply enjoy seeing the world through a different perspective, I'm glad you're here.

This is my journey — from a camera in my hands to stories captured through my lens.

And this is only the beginning.

I'm Rahul, a photographer from Kochi, Kerala. Welcome to my world, one frame at a time.
